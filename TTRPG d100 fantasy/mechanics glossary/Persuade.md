**Persuade** - (charm/edu) - the ability to persuade people and talk them into doing things for you, either convincing them to agree to your side of an argument, or give you discounts for example. Charm or Edu can be used as the base characteristic for this skill, as both could be used to persuade people in various ways. Either convince them with your charm, or help them see reason.  ^af76af

**Performance()**- (charm) -this skill is used to perform skills related to performing arts. Music, theater, dance are all associated with performance. A performance check can be used to try and gain some coin by utilizing your talents, it could also be used to attempt to gain the favour of or charm an individual to have a better disposition towards you.  ^e8e8dc

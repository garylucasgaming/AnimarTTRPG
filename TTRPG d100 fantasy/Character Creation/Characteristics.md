## Characteristics

characteristic scores are the foundation of your character. All skills are based off of the fundamental ability scores of your character. 

The main abilities are: 

**Strength, Dexterity, Fortitude,  Intellect, Education, Charm**

to determine your ability scores, you roll 2d10, and add 20. this represents your characters score. the 1's column indicates your characters ability modifier. 


> [!Tip]+ Example
> Johnny rolls 2d10 to determine his characters strength.  he rolls a total of 17. he adds 20, and his characters strength is 37. 
> The 1's column indicates his character's strength modifier. so 3 in this case.

### Class Prestige's:

Prestige's let you focus your build in certain directions, letting you become an expert in a specific area for your build.  you gain new prestige features at levels 8, 13, 18. 

#### Scoundrel
when you take this prestige you gain [[Weapon Skill]]() of your choice, [[Knowledge]]() of your choice, and [[Survival]] as a trained skill. 

Scoundrels gain the **Fast hands** ability, allowing them to to use tools and disarm kits as bonus actions, as well as manipulate and use objects as a bonus action. Also gain the [[Parry]] talent. 

###### Strategist
you gain this ability at level 3. once per turn, the scoundrel can choose from a list of abilities to apply. 

###### Strategist
you gain this ability at level 8. once per turn, the scoundrel can choose from a list of abilities to apply.

| defensive roll   | as a reaction, if an opponent would hit the rogue with a melee attack, the scoundrel can roll an [[Evasion Skill]] with +20, to move up to 5 ft and take half damage.(uncanny dodge does not effect this) |

###### Gang Up
you gain this ability at level 13.  when you are [[Flanking]] an opponent, you can use a bonus action to make an attack with your main hand. 

###### Feature 3
you gain this ability at level 18. 

#### Assassin
Assassins are masters in the art of the kill. There is no greater honour than pulling off a successful hit cleanly and without witnesses. Ideally the perfect hit is carried out in a way that the target will have seemed to have died of natural causes. But don't be mistaken, if you corner an assassin, be prepared for a fight. 

when you take this prestige  gain, [[Weapon Skill]]() of your choice, [[Knowledge]](poisons), and [[Medicine]] as trained skills. gain the poisoners kit as well.

###### Ghost Movement
you gain this ability at level 8. The assassin learns to blend into the shadows. gain a +10 bonus to [[Hide]] and [[move silently]] checks, as well as enemies gain a -10 on [[Perception]] tests to discover you while hidden. 

###### Assassinate
you gain this ability at level 13. when you make a sneak attack it always counts as a devastating hit. 

###### retreat to the shadows
you gain this ability at level 18. 

#### Duelist
gain [[Weapon Skill]]() of your choice, [[Use Rope]], and [[Navigate]] as trained skills. 

Duelists also no longer suffer a penalty for using a light weapon in their offhand to make an attack. as well as duelists gain the [[Parry]] talent. 

###### Can't Escape
you gain this ability at level 3. you can use a bonus action to make an attack with your offhand to try and lock up an opponents weapon. if you succeed an opposing [[Weapon Skill]] test at -10, your next attack against that opponent cannot be evaded, or parried. 

###### Can't Escape
you gain this ability at level 8. you can use a bonus action to make an attack with your offhand to try and lock up an opponents weapon. if you succeed an opposing [[Weapon Skill]] test at -10, your next attack against that opponent cannot be evaded, or parried. 

###### Duelist Advance
you gain this ability at level 13.  After making a successful attack, you may expend a bonus action to move your opponent 5ft backwards. 

###### Master Duelist
you gain this ability at level 18. 



### Class Abilities:

#### Traps Use
Traps use lets you use a [[Perception]] test to detect traps. If you own a Trap disarm kit, you may also make a [[Knowledge]](traps) to disarm traps. 

#### Sneak Attack
if a rogue targets an enemy that is unaware of them, by being hidden, moving silently or other means, then the rogue may use an action to make an attack that gets +10 to hit, they may choose the hit location, and it counts as 1 severity higher for damage. 

#### Uncanny dodge
rogues possess the ability to making daring escapes. When an attack requires a [[Evasion Skill]] test to reduce damage. if the rogue succeeds, they take no damage instead. 

#### retreat to the shadows
after making a sneak attack, you can use a bonus action to move half of your movement and make a [[Hide]] test. 


#### Weak spot
you can spend a bonus action to study an opponent. you gain a +5 bonus on your next attack against that opponent.

### Class Abilities:

#### Characteristics Boost
at level 1 Rogues gets to add 1d10 to a [[Hide]], [[move silently]],  and [[Dexterity]]. 

#### Traps Use
Traps use lets you use a [[Perception]] test to detect traps. If you own a Trap disarm kit, you may also make a [[Knowledge]](traps) to disarm traps. 

#### Sneak Attack
if a rogue targets an enemy that is unaware of them, by being hidden, moving silently or other means, then the rogue may use an action to make an attack that gets +10 to hit, they may choose the hit location, and roll the damage from the weapon twice taking the higher roll.  

#### Greater Sneak Attack
When making a Sneak attack, roll the damage from the weapon twice adding them together. 

#### Master Sneak Attack
When making a sneak attack roll the damage from the weapon 3 times adding them together. 

#### Uncanny dodge
rogues possess the ability to making daring escapes. When an attack requires a [[Evasion Skill]] test to reduce damage. if the rogue succeeds, they take no damage instead. 

#### Retreat to the shadows
after making a sneak attack, you can use a bonus action to move half of your movement and make a [[Hide]] test. 


#### Weak spot
you can spend a bonus action to study an opponent. you gain a +5 bonus on your next attack against that opponent.

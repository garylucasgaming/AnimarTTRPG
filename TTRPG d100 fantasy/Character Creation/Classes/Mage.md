### Known Spells

Mages learn spells through study and practice. Starting at level 1, a mage knows as many spells as their [[Intellect]] modifier + 2. Each time a mage levels up, they gain a number of new spells equal to their intellect modifier.

A mages level determines what level of spells they can cast. Every two levels starting from level 1,  a mage can learn a new level of spell. For example, level 1 mage can learn level 1 spells. a level 3 mage can learn level 2 spells. 5=3, 7=4, 9=5, 11=6, 13=7, 15=8, 17=9.

### Class Titles


When you create a mage you must choose a magical discipline. Your magical discipline will determine your Mana source as well spells your class will have easy access to. 

Magical disciplines include: 

####  Blood Mage
blood mages use the power of their own blood, and sometimes others, to harness the raw magical energy inherent within all creatures. the more potent the blood, the more potent the mana that can be generated from it. this leads to some obvious downsides, the more powerful spells you attempt to cast, the more life is needed. 

- gain 1 **Blood amulet**. 

Blood mages start with a Blood Amulet. this amulet is a storage device for the magical essence found in blood. it is the focus for all their spells. Whenever blood is dropped onto the amulet, it will infuse mana based on the creatures mana sphere whos blood was used. The amount of blood the blood amulet can hold is equal to the level of the blood mage/2(rounded up)  plus the [[Intellect]] Modifier of the blood mage. So at level 1, it would be 1+ intellect modifier.  

Blood mages also start with the **Blood Sacrifice** Ability. you can spend 1 bonus action to sacrifice some of your blood or the blood of a willing or helpless(or freshly dead) creature to generate 1 mana of that creatures related mana sphere. 

###### Mana Spheres: 
blood mages start with access to Death, Illusion, and Destruction mana spheres.

##### Discipline abilities

At level 3 Blood mages gain the ability to create **Blood Homunculi**. as a standard action, by expending 3 blood of the same type from their amulet, they can create a small creature who shares the same blood type(thus the same mana type) of the blood used. Blood homunculi are always willing to share their blood with the blood mage. A blood homunculi lasts for 24 hours before collapsing into a pile of coagulated blood. only one blood homunculi of each blood type can be sustained by the amulet at any time. 

At level 8 Blood Mages gain the **Siphon Blood** Ability. this is a standard action, and it lets the blood mage extract blood from those around it. each non willing creature within 15 feet must roll a [[Fortitude]] test or the blood mage gets to drain some of their blood. this blood is immediately transferred into the blood amulet. 

At level 13  Blood mages gain the ability **Blood Explosion**. as a standard action, they can make a single blood homunculi explode, dealing 1d10 damage to each creature in 5ft. this also returns the 3 blood used to create the homunculi back to the amulet.

#### Rune Mage
Rune mages use the study of runes and their ancient rituals taught by the great dwarven rune smiths. Able to harness mana and coalesce it into a single entity known as a rune.  While runes are a consistent way to cast spells, runes become very convoluted and complex the more powerful they become, making the adjustment to powerful spells a little trickier. 

Rune mages start with a **Rune hammer**. this hammer is intricate and ornate and is enchanted with the ability to inscribe runes into the air itself. this is how rune mages cast spells. 

Rune mages also start with 2 basic **Rune Talisman**. a basic rune talisman has the ability to store mana of a single type and can hold 3 mana. 

Rune mages gain the ability **Infuse Rune**. which lets them trace over the rune of a talisman to charge it. this is a bonus action but will add 1 mana of the talismans type. 

###### Mana spheres: 
Rune mages start with Creation plus any 2 mana spheres. 

##### Discipline abilities

At level 3 rune mages gain [[Craft]](Rune Stones) as a trained skill. Rune stones can be used to store spells by infusing the mana needed into the stone itself. rune stones can only be used once and are destroyed in the process of using them. 

At level 8 rune mages gain [[Craft]](runic talisman) as a trained skill. 

At level 13  rune mages gain the **RuneSmith** ability. this makes it so that whenever a rune mage casts a channeled spell, they do not need to concentrate on that spell for subsequent turns. the amount of channeled spells they can have going is equal to their [[Intellect]] modifier / 3.   the spells still consume mana each turn they are active.  

At level 18 rune mages gain the **RuneLord** ability. when using rune stones, there is a 50% chance they are not destroyed when used. the runesmith ability is now based on [[Intellect]] modifier/2. and when casting a buff spell that affects an ally, they may choose an additional ally to affect.

#### Witch
witches use the power of the ethereal realm to harness their spellcasting abilities. they do this through the use of a familiar. Their familiar is their conduit, and the stronger their familiar grows the more powerful the witch grows too. keeping your familiar alive and protecting it becomes the most important thing for a witch, as losing your familiar loses you access to your mana source. 

Witches gain a **Familiar**. this familiar can be any normal tiny animal like a Cat, rat, bat, frog, newt, etc.    

This familiar is magically linked to the witch. they can speak telepathically, and the familiar shares an [[Intellect]] score with its master.  As a witch becomes more powerful so too does its familiar. being able to cast spells for the witch as well as counting as an additional caster for ritual spells. 

Familiars generate mana for the witch. As a standard action a familiar can commune with dark energies. this will generate 2 mana within the witches mana spheres. A familiar can only store mana equal to the Witches level times 2. 

###### Mana Spheres: 
witches start with access to Shadow, Earth, and Light Mana Spheres. 

##### Discipline abilities

At level 3 a witches familiar gains the ability to cast one of the witches level 1 spells. Witches also gain the ability to cast **Hexes**. A witch can only maintain 1 hex at a time.

### Class Abilities:
#### Characteristics boost
at level 1, the mage gets to add 1d10 to [[Arcane Arts]] and [[Intellect]]. 

#### Mana Charge
all mages have the ability to charge mana. while this might not be the most efficient way to generate mana, it is a way to get access to mana one might not be able to generate normally.  As a standard action, the mage taps into the well force of magic that permeates all living things. they are able to focus and harness a small amount of that mana to be used. Without storage, the mana will disappear after 1 turn. Generate 1 mana of a type of your choice

#### Mana Siphon
Mages can eventually learn the ability to siphon mana directly from objects or people. As a standard action, the mage can focus in on mana that is floating or stored inside an object. they can then siphon that mana and add it to their own.  For example, if a blood mage holds a powerful runic talisman, they can mana siphon 1 stored mana from within the talisman to use for themselves. Conversely, if two mages are combating one another, and a mage generates mana using mana charge, another mage could attempt to mana siphon that mana for themselves.    Mana siphon is an [[Arcane Arts]] test, and is an opposed one when trying to siphon mana from an unwilling creature. 



#### Counter spell
at level 4 mages discover a deeper understanding for interacting with other mages in combat.  As a reaction, when an opposing mage casts a spell, the player may spend 1 mana of any type to attempt to counterspell. after the opposing mage has rolled for the suess of their spell, the player may make an opposing [[Arcane Arts]] test to attempt to counter the spell. if they succeed the spell fizzles out. The player may attempt a [[Knowledge]](arcane arts) test beforehand to determine the mana sphere of the spell, in which case if they expend the same type of mana as spell being cast, they get a +10 on their [[Arcane Arts]] opposing test.

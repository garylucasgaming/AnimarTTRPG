## Magic

there are 16 mana spheres where all magic and mana derive from.

- Fire
- Water
- Earth
- Air
- Shadow
- Light
- Death
- Life
- Divination
- Illusion
- Time
- Space
- Void
- creation
- destruction
- universal

The spheres are connected in ways, that allow easy access to other spheres. Not all spheres are connected, but that does not mean they cannot be accessed. 

When choosing your spellcasting class, each class will have different spheres they gain access too. This will determine the spells that class can cast.  As you level up, you'll have opportunities to gain access to different spheres, but those which are connected will come more easily. 


### Mana Sources

Each class will have different ways to generate and store mana. This mana is then used to cast spells. For instance, a blood mage uses blood. the mana they gain is depended on the mana sphere that is related to the creature whos blood they are using to charge their blood amulet. A [[Dwarf]] for instance, is related to the Creation Mana sphere. Thus when a blood mage uses dwarf blood, they will generate Creation mana.  ^ce61aa

Spells from the universal sphere can use any type of mana to be cast. The universal sphere encompasses all magic, and despite the techniques used by certain mages, universal magic is easily tapped into by all. 


### casting spells

each spell will have its own casting requirements. Verbal, somatic, casting time, Mana spent, and depending on the spell type, might require differing components. 

#### Types of spells
there are 3 types of spells. **Ritual**, **Channeled**, and an **Evoked** Spell. 

##### Ritual spells
ritual spells require intricate set up.  They usually require some kind of ritual circle to be created, of which the spell will denote time in which to create said circle.  they will also require special ritual items like candles, or maybe skulls. Each ritual is different. once the circle and items are sufficient, a chant is usually performed by one or more casters to perform the spell. The more Casters chanting the higher the success chance usually. A ritual spell will denote the bonus for each additional caster. Note that non-casters can help cast a ritual spell as well, however the bonus might not be that significant. 

##### Channeled spells
Channeled spells can only be performed by a single caster. The main difference between a channeled spell and a Ritual Spell is that a channeled spell does not require a circle. A channeled spell usually persists over multiple turns, using up mana each turn to stay active. 

##### Evoked Spells
Evoked spells are instantaneous effect spells.  These spells usually have less of an impact as channeled or ritual spells, but being able to cast them in a singular turn gives them some advantages over the other types of spells, especially in combat.

### Casting spells higher than your level


All spellcasters can attempt to cast spells of higher level,  given that they either A) have those spells already known(through heightening a spell on their current spell list), or B) have a written version that is castable/ consumable that they can translate. (i.e a rune stone). 

When attempting to cast a spell of higher level, a spell caster must roll a [[Arcane Arts]] roll. for each spell level above their current spell level they are able to cast, there is a -20 applied to the roll. they cannot gain benefits of talents or class abilities when casting spells of higher level unless stated otherwise.  A roll of a 1, will always succeed, and a roll of a 95-100 will always fail.

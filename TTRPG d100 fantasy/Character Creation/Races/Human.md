#### Human Versatility
humans inherently are jacks of all trades. As such, pick 2 basic [[Skills]], they become trained.  Also pick two [[Characteristics]], roll a d10 for each and add the result to them. 

### Kingdoms:

Humans come from all sorts of parts of the world. depending on which kingdom they are from, they have various differing abilities. 

#### Altay
The kingdom of Altay is home to the many nobles and aristocrats of the humans in the north. Altay developed itself as a seat of power amongst humans, and openly trades with the elves to the east and dwarves to the north taking influence from both cultures, and being very prosperous in the same right. 

Altayan humans gain 1 trained [[Knowledge]]() skill to denote their study at the various academies in the Altayan Cities. 

#### Naryn
The tribes of the naryn hills live a more nomadic life than their cousins to the north. They roam the lands following the herds of buffalo and animals to hunt, living a less industrialized life than the Altayans. 

Naryn Tribesman gain [[Survival]] as a trained skill to denote their life in the hills and plains of Naryn.

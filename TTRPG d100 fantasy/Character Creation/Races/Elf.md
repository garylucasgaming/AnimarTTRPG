# Elf

![[Pasted image 20250101015616.png|300]]

## Racial traits: 

Creature type: humanoid
size: medium
speed: dexterity  ft/round
languages: common, elf
mana sphere: earth
Hit point modifier:  -2 Hp

#### Low Light Vision
Elves can see up to 60ft with little light. 

#### Elven Grace
Elves are [[resistant]] to charm spells and enchantment spells that try to force an action upon the elf.  

#### Elven Ears
Elves have impressive hearing. they gain [[Listen]] as a trained skill.   

#### Elven Intellect
Elves have lived for many years, and have gained much knowledge and wisdom.  Roll 1d10, and add the result to either [[Intellect]] or [[Education]].

### Subraces:

#### Rakau
The Rakau elves live in the northern Rakau forest. They believe nature is the most important part of creation, and strive to protect it. They also believe they were created to use its resources for good, and create a symbiotic bond with nature to guard it from outsiders. They were able to harness the powers of the earth to create the first understandings of magic, and used it to shape their kingdom, and enchant many magical items in the golden age. 

Rakau elves gain 2 [[Knowledge]]() trained skills. 

#### Whatitiri
![[Pasted image 20250101015733.png|300]]
The Whatitiri elves live in the southern forest of Rakau. They split ideologically from their northern counterparts due to differing beliefs in how to preserve and protect Nature. The Whatitiri belive the forest to be sacred, and refrain from using the resources the tree goddess they worship gives them. Instead they hold the trees of the forest in reverence and lift them to an exalted status. Using the animals in the forest for whatever they need instead. Wearing armor made of leather, and tools made of bone, these elves typically live in the canopies of trees or in natural caves. They are also strictly carnivorous. 

as a whatitiri elf, gain [[Survival]] and [[Handle Animal]] as a trained skill. 

#### Raki Ngahere
![[Pasted image 20250101020517.png|300]]
These elves were close to the ancient dwarves during the cataclysm. The magical sundering of the dwarven empire rippled out and corrupted the northern forests that were the Raki Ngahere's home. Over millennia the forests twisted and decayed, turning much into dense and disturbing swamps.  This took a toll on these elves, slowly darkening their skin to an ashen grey. 

as a Raki Ngahere elf gain [[Intimidate]] and [[Deceive]] as a trained skill.

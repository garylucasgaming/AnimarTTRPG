## Racial traits: 

Creature type: humanoid
size: medium

languages: common, dwarf
mana sphere: creation
Hit point modifier:  +2 hp

#### Darkvision
Dwarves can see in the dark up to 30ft 

#### Grudges
Dwarves are notoriously good at keeping grudges. any creatures who have fought them, or done wrong by them get thrown into a list of grudges. Each dwarf keeps their own list, but the most heinous crimes against dwarves get recorded on the gilded throne in Konig Hold. Whenever you defeat an enemy creature whose type you've recorded in your list of grudges, you become [[Bolstered]] for 1 turn. 

#### Resilient
dwarves are a sturdy race, they are [[resistant]] to intoxication and poison effects. Also, Roll 1d10 and add the result to [[Fortitude]]

### Subraces:

#### Mountain Dwarf
the mountain dwarves of Konig Hold under the World's Peak Mountain are an industrious and laborious group. They have cemented the longest standing kingdom of dwarves that has lasted millennia through the rich history of the many great Dwarven Houses.  Remnants of the ancient dwarven civilization that torn asunder in the cataclysm, these dwarves have persevered through it all. 

as a mountain dwarf gain 1 trained [[Craft]]() skill of your choice, as well as the [[Appraise]]() skill. 
When appraising stonework or metalwork, mountain dwarves gain a +5 modifier. 

#### Hill Dwarf
The hill dwarves, scattered throughout animar, are cousins of the mountain dwarves. they form small clans and live under hills. They breed pigs and warboars for both eating and riding into battle.  while not as skilled at craftmanship as their cousins, they have a tenacity that is unmatched. 

as a hill dwarf, gain [[First Aid]] and [[Handle Animal]] as trained skills. And gain a +5 modifier when using handle animal on Swine.

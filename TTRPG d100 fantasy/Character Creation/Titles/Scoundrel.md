### Description: 

when you take this Title you gain [[Weapon Skill]]() of your choice, [[Knowledge]]() of your choice, and [[Survival]] as a trained skill. 

Scoundrels gain the **Fast hands** ability, 

###### Fast Hands
you can to use tools and disarm kits as bonus actions, as well as manipulate and use objects as a bonus action.

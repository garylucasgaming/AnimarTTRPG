### Description: 

blood mages use the power of their own blood, and sometimes others, to harness the raw magical energy inherent within all creatures. the more potent the blood, the more potent the mana that can be generated from it. this leads to some obvious downsides, the more powerful spells you attempt to cast, the more life is needed. 

Upon selecting this as a Title gain: 

 gain 1 **Blood amulet**. 

Blood mages start with a Blood Amulet. this amulet is a storage device for the magical essence found in blood. it is the focus for all their spells. Whenever blood is dropped onto the amulet, it will infuse mana based on the creatures mana sphere whos blood was used. The amount of blood the blood amulet can hold is equal to the level of the blood mage/2(rounded up)  plus the [[Intellect]] Modifier of the blood mage. So at level 1, it would be 1+ intellect modifier.  

Blood mages also start with the **Blood Sacrifice** Ability. 

###### Blood Sacrifice
you can spend 1 bonus action to sacrifice some of your blood or the blood of a willing or helpless(or freshly dead) creature to generate 1 mana of that creatures related mana sphere. 


##### Mana Sphere
Blood mages start with Death, Illusion, and Destruction mana spheres.

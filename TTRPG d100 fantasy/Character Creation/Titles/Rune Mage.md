### Description: 

Rune mages use the study of runes and their ancient rituals taught by the great dwarven rune smiths. Able to harness mana and coalesce it into a single entity known as a rune.  While runes are a consistent way to cast spells, runes become very convoluted and complex the more powerful they become, making the adjustment to powerful spells a little trickier. 

Rune mages start with a [[Rune hammer]]. this hammer is intricate and ornate and is enchanted with the ability to inscribe runes into the air itself. this is how rune mages cast spells. 

Rune mages also start with 2 basic [[Rune Talisman]]. a basic rune talisman has the ability to store mana of a single type and can hold 3 mana. 

Rune mages gain the ability Infuse Rune.

###### Infuse Rune
you can trace over the rune of a talisman with your [[Rune hammer]] to charge it. this is a bonus action but will add 1 mana of the talismans type. 

###### Mana spheres: 
Rune mages start with Death plus any 2 mana spheres.

### Description: 

witches use the power of the ethereal realm to harness their spellcasting abilities. they do this through the use of a familiar. Their familiar is their conduit, and the stronger their familiar grows the more powerful the witch grows too. keeping your familiar alive and protecting it becomes the most important thing for a witch, as losing your familiar loses you access to your mana source. 

Witches gain a [[#Familiar]]. this familiar can be any normal tiny animal like a Cat, rat, bat, frog, newt, etc.    



###### Mana Spheres: 
witches start with access to Shadow, Earth, and Light Mana Spheres. 


### Familiar
This familiar is magically linked to the witch. they can speak telepathically, and the familiar shares an [[Intellect]] score with its master.  As a witch becomes more powerful so too does its familiar. being able to cast spells for the witch as well as counting as an additional caster for ritual spells. 

Familiars act as their own creatures and have their own actions each turn. controlled by the with player. 

Familiars count as casters for the purpose of ritual spells, and are always willing to help a witch with ritual spells. 

Every 5 levels, starting from level 5, a witch gains another familiar. 

###### Commune With Darkness
Familiars gain this ability. As a standard action a familiar can commune with dark energies. this will generate 2 mana within the witches mana spheres which it can then store within itself. A familiar can only store mana equal to the Witches level times 2.

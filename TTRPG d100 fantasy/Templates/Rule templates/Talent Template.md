#### Shield Block
Prerequisite: `=this.requisite`

when you are hit with an attack that does physical damage, you may use your reaction for the turn to block with your shield. Any damage you would take is reduced by your shields hardness. Any remaining damage is deal to the shield. if the shield is destroyed by this action, any remaining damage afterwards is dealt to you.

## Crafting 

Crafting is used for many purposes in this game. 

Generally, you main gain a crafting skill anytime you could gain a new [[Craft]]() skill. Though some [[Craft]]() skills may be locked behind certain feats. 


For instance, while you could take [[Craft]](Swords) at level 1 without a feat, you would need a feat to gain access to [[Craft]](Rune Stones). 

Anytime a feat is associated with a crafting skill, you are not able to take that crafting skill outside of taking that feat. unless otherwise stated in some other form(like in a class or title). 

for crafting specific items mentioned in feats, first refer to the feat itself, any rules mentioned in the crafting process of the feat will trump any rules written here. 

###### Before you craft
To craft an item you will need required materials and **1/2 the value** of the item in gold. Depending on the quality and item type a certain amount of time is needed to craft an item as well. 

| Item Type    | Days to craft                               |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Weapon       | 4 days per weight category                  |
| Armor        | 7 days per weight category                  |
| Crafts       | 1 day                                       |
| General Gear | 1 day                                       |
| Furniture    | 7 days                                      |
| Stonework    | 7 days                                      |
| Clothing     | 3 days per size category(starting at small) |


###### Crafting Roll
for each day it takes to craft an item. you must make a [[Craft]]() roll of the associated skill. 
on a success nothing changes and the crafting moves forward. On a failure, the time needed to craft the item increases by 1 day.  When you succeed, if you get 4 degrees of success, the time needed to craft the item reduces by 1 day.



## Rune Talismans

Rune talismans can store magic inside of them. By inscribing the correct rune onto the stone for a type of mana, the talisman can then be charged with mana of the corresponding type. 

rune talismans are more difficult to craft based on the amount of mana wanting to be stored. 

| Talisman type | Mana Stored |
| ------------- | ----------- |
| Basic         | 3           |
| Expert        | 5           |
| Advanced      | 8           |
| Master        | 12          |
| Dual*         | 5/5         |
| Advanced Dual | 8/8         |
| Master Dual   | 12/12       |
*a specific talent is required to craft dual talismans

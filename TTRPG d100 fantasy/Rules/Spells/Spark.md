 **Spark** 
 Level: 1
 Type: evoked, Air
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 1 air
 Target: 1 creature
 Range: 50ft. 
 Duration: instantaneous
 casting time: standard action

a jolt of lightning shoots out and hits the target dealing 1d5-2 damage. if there is another creature within 5 feet of the target the spark jumps to that creature as well dealing 1d5-3.

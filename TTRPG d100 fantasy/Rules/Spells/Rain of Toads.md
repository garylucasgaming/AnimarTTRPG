 **Rain of Toads** 
 Level: 1
 Type: Ritual, life
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 2 life
 Target: 10ft circle
 Range: 30ft. 
 Duration: 3 turns
 casting time: 2 turns

 toads rain from the sky pummeling anything within the radius of the circle. any creature that starts their turn in the circle takes 1d5 [[Bludgeoning]] damage.

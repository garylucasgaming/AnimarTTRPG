 **Shield** 
 Level: 1
 Type: evoked, creation
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 1 creation
 Target: 1 willing creature or self
 Range: 30ft. 
 Duration: 10 minutes
 casting time: standard action

the target gains emits a magical aura which protects them from a single attack. The next time the target is hit with an attack that does physical damage the damage is reduced by 6 and the shield spell ends.

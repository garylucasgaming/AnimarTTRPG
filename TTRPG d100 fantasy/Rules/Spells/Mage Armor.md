 **Mage Armor** 
 Level: 1
 Type: evoked, Creation
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 2 creation
 Target: self
 Range: self. 
 Duration: 10 minutes
casting time: standard action

 until the spell ends, magical armor envelopes your person and you are protected from physical attacks. Mage armor counts as Wearing Leather armor however it does not impede your ability to cast spells.

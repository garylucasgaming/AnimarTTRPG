 **Alarm** 
 Level: 1
 Type: evoked, universal
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 1 any
 Target: 30 foot sphere
 Range: self. 
 Duration: 8 hours
 casting time: standard action

 anything that enters the 30 foot sphere after casting will trigger an alarm that goes off mentally inside the head of the caster. Alerting them that something has entered the sphere. 

Specific conditions can be applied such as:
- any creatures currently in the sphere will not trigger the alarm if they leave and reenter. 
- specific sizes of creatures will not trigger the alarm
- only specific types of creatures will trigger the alarm

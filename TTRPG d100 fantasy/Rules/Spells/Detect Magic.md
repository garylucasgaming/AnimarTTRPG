 **Detect Magic**
 Level: 1
 Type: evoked, Universal
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 1 any
 Target: self
 Range: self 
 Duration: 1 minute
 casting time: standard action

 you gain the ability to detect magical sources with your sight. Anything that stores mana or spells, is inscribed with a magical rune, that has innate magical abilities, or otherwise is magic can be seen by you within your normal sight range. this does not give you xray abilities, but if there is something magical nearby that is blocked by an object, you have a feeling that something magical is in that direction. you can make a [[Knowledge]]([[Arcane Arts]]) test to determine the type of magic a source is emitting.

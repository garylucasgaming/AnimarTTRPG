 **Tremor** 
 Level: 1
 Type: Ritual, earth
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 1 earth
 Target: 1 creature
 Range: 30ft. 
 Duration: 3 turns
casting time: 2 turns

 you point to the target creature and the ground beneath their feet trembles and softens. the target must make a [[Dexterity]] test or sink 1 feet into the ground. each turn the target must make a [[Strength]] test with -5 for each foot sank, or sink 1 additional feet. once the spell duration ends the target can make a [[Strength]] test with -5 to the roll for each foot sunk,  to escape the earth.

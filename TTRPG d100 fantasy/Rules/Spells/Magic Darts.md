 **Magic Darts**
 Level: 1
 Type: evoked, Creation
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 1 creation
 Target: 1 creature
 Range: 50ft. 
 casting time: standard action
 during: instantaneous

 3 magical darts shoot out from your hand and towards your target. each dart deals 1d5-2 (minimum 1) force damage. 

overcharged: (maximum of level)
for each creation mana outside the first used to cast this spell. add 1 additional dart. 
double casting time.

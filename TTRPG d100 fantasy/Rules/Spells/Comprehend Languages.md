 **Comprehend Languages** 
 Level: 1
 Type: evoked, universal
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 1 any
 Target: self
 Range: self
 Duration: 10 minutes
 casting time: standard action

 you can understand any verbal languages spoken to you. you also understand the written language of any language but you must be touching the manuscript or source of writing and it takes 1 minute per page to decipher.

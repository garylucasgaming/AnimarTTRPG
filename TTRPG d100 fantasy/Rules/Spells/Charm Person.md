 **Charm Person** 
 Level: 1
 Type: evoked, illusion
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 1 illusion
 Target: 1 creature
 Range: 15ft. 
 Duration: instantaneous
 casting time: standard action

 target must make a [[Intellect]] test or become charmed. while charmed the target will act friendly towards you and will susceptible to your suggestions. the target will not do anything that would harm it or anything that would conflict morally with its own morality, however actions the target wouldn't normally do, but would do for an ally it will do for you.

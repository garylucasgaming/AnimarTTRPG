 **Fire Bolt** 
 Level: 1
 Type: evoked, fire
 Casters: 1
 Components: V, S
 Mana: 1 fire
 Target: 1 creature
 Range: 50ft. 
 Duration: instantaneous
 casting time: standard action

fires a bolt of fire out that hits the target dealing 1d5-1 fire damage. roll 1d100 on a roll of 15 or less the target is set on fire(assuming they have flammable clothes).

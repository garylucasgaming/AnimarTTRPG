## Wounds and Treatment

throughout combat, wounds are inevitable. They will cause your character negative side affects if left untreated, and more serious wounds can severely hinder your characters ability. 


There are three types of wounds in this game, Minor Wounds, Major Wounds, and Critical Wounds. 

Minor wounds range from light burns to small cuts. Nothing too serious but if left untreated could cause some complications. 

Major wounds are wounds that need to be treated as soon as possible or your character could develop serious conditions. Major burns, lacerations, broken and fractured limbs,. etc. 

Critical wounds, are wounds that need to be treated immediately or death is inevitable. Loss of limbs, disembowling, complete immolation. These things must be dealt with as soon as possible. 


Each body part can only sustain a certain amount of each type of wound. 


| Body part | Minor Wounds | Major Wounds | Critical Wounds | Total Wounds |
| --------- | ------------ | ------------ | --------------- | ------------ |
| Head      | 3            | 1            | 1               | 3            |
| Body      | 4            | 2            | 1               | 4            |
| Arms      | 4            | 2            | 1               | 4            |
| Legs      | 4            | 2            | 1               | 4            |

As you can see, there is a total number of wounds a limb can take. If you have reached that many wounds, and you take another wound. the wound is automatically a critical wound. 

The head cannot sustain as many wounds. As well as Critical wounds to the head have a chance for immediate death. so be VERY careful when protecting your head. 


### First Aid 

This leads us to the first line of defense. [[First Aid]]. 

this skill is incredibly useful at patching up wounds and making sure you're able to survive until more long term treatment is sought out. 

With all [[First Aid]] saves, you must succeed on a roll. Depending on the type of wound the difficulty of such tasks could increase. 

Different implements and tools could help with [[First Aid]], giving you a much needed bonus when performing such duties. (see Gear)


| Wound          | Difficulty    | example                                                                   |
| -------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| minor wound    | Difficult -10 | applying pressure to a small cut.                                         |
| Major Wound    | Hard -20      | making a splint for a dislocated knee.                                    |
| Critical Wound | Very Hard -30 | creating a makeshift tourniquet to stop the flow of blood to a lost hand. |


### Medicine

The [[Medicine]] Skill is used for basic doctoring. It can be used for applying ointments and tinctures, dressing and caring for wounds, treatment of fevers and infections, stitching wounds, the list goes on. 

What [[Medicine]] is not able to do is reattach limbs or do complex surgery. 

A patient after receiving first aid, must then receive a [[Medicine]] check(usually done by another player) every day for an amount of days depending on the type of wound. 

| days | wound type |
| ---- | ---------- |
| 1    | minor      |
| 7    | major      |
| 14   | critical   |

A Character is incapacitated for 1/4 of the time needed to recover except for minor wounds. Wounds however have compounding effects. If you have multiple wounds. the days are added together instead of overlapping.  so if you have 4 minor wounds, and 1 major wound. you would need 11 days to recover. 

This time can be sped up with Good Medicine checks. each day, when a medicine check is made, if 3 degrees of success are made, the time to recover is decreased by 1 day. if 4+ degrees of success, the time to recover is shortened by two days. Though the time to recover can never be brought below half of the original amount. 

A failure on the medicine check, adds a day to recovery, as well is increases the likelihood of infection. 

### Infection

Infection can set in, if a major or critical wound is not treated with antibacterial ointments. (i.e pinesap, iodine etc. ).

if left untreated, or a medicine check is failed, each day the chance of infection increases by 25%. after the medicine check is rolled. a infection roll is made. 

As long as the medicine check was succeeded, and they had the appropriate tools to disinfect the patient, then the chance for infection goes to zero. 

If a wound becomes infected, each day the character gains 1 point of infection. The amount of infection points CANNOT exceed the characters [[Fortitude]] modifier. if it does the characters fever becomes too high, and they will decease. 

Once a wound is infected, a Successful medicine check, 2 days in a row, is enough to remove the infection. 

### Limb Loss

Some wounds are tough to come back from. Will you may survive, and recover, losing a limb is unparalleled.  

There are powerful magics that are able to bring limbs back, however those usually take multiple days to perform by powerful spellcasters. Thus the cost is usually astronomical. 

Another alternative is crafting or purchasing prosthetics. Usually made from magically enchanted metals. though mundane versions exist as well. (you can find these under Gear)

Loss of certain limbs has specific drawbacks.

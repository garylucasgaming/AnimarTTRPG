#### Shield Bash
Prerequisite: `=this.requisite`

you may use your shield as weapon.  Gain [[Weapon Skill]](shields) as a trained skill. 

A shield weapon profile looks like this: 

Weapon name:  shield
Damage Type: [[Bludgeoning]]
Hands: one Handed
Characteristic: Strength
weapon type: shield
weapon weight: medium
reach: 5ft
Damage: 1d5
Weapon effect: 
if you have 2 degrees of success or more, the target must make a [[Dexterity]] save or be pushed 5 ft directly backwards.

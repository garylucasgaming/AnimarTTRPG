#### Craft rune stone
Prerequisite: `=this.requisite`

gain the ability to craft [[Rune Stone]]s


rune stones are more difficult to craft based on the level of spell inscribed onto the stone. 

Mana needed to cast the spell must be used to create the rune. if you do not have access to the mana needed you cannot craft the stone with that specific spell. 

you must know the spell you are going to craft into the rune stone and you must be able to cast the spell level. 

| spell level | time     | cost in gp | craft dc |
| ----------- | -------- | ---------- | -------- |
| 1           | 1 day    | 25         | +0       |
| 2           | 3 days   | 100        | -5       |
| 3           | 1 week   | 225        | -10      |
| 4           | 2 weeks  | 400        | -15      |
| 5           | 4 weeks  | 625        | -20      |
| 6           | 8 weeks  | 900        | -25      |
| 7           | 16 weeks | 1225       | -30      |
| 8           | 32 weeks | 1600       | -35      |
| 9           | 48 weeks | 2025       | -40      |


to craft a [[Rune Stone]] roll an [[Arcane Arts]] and use the craft dc  to determine success or failure. you must have the [[Craft Rune Stone]] talent to craft rune stones. 

a failure on the crafting check doubles the time and money needed to craft a [[Rune Stone]]

#### Counter Spell Knowledge
Prerequisite: `=this.requisite`

The player may attempt a [[Knowledge]](arcane arts) test before using Counter Spell to determine the mana sphere of the spell, in which case if they expend the same type of mana as spell being cast, they get a +10 on their [[Arcane Arts]] opposing test.

#### Ritual Caster
Prerequisite: `=this.requisite`

you may cast evoked spells as ritual spells. if you do the casting time for the spell is equal to the level of the spell / 2 in turns. (a level 1 spell would be 1 turn(meaning it goes off at the beginning of your second turn casting the spell), and a level 6 spell would be 3 turns). 

when casting an evoked spell as a ritual spell, you may choose 1 aspect of the spell and increase it. 

- if you choose target, increase the number of targets by 1.
- if you choose [[Skill Test]], the target gets a -10 on their skill test against the effect of the spell. 
- if you choose damage, increase the damage of the spell by your [[Intellect]] modifier
- if you choose range, double the range. (if the spells range is self you may choose any target within 5ft)

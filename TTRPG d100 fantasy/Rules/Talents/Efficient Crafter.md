#### Efficient Crafter
Prerequisite: `=this.requisite`

while crafting, when you  make your craft roll for each day. the cost of the item is reduced by the amount of days it takes to craft the item below the initial starting number. Meaning if you successfully reduce the crafting time of an item, you can reduce the cost of the item. 

For each day you reduce the crafting time by, you are refunded, 5% of the value you paid to craft the item.

#### Craft Rune Stones
Prerequisite: `=this.requisite`

Gain [[Craft]](Rune Talisman) as a trained skill. [[Rune Talisman]] are used to store mana which can be used to cast spells. A rune Talisman can only hold 1 type of mana. 



| Talisman type | Mana Stored | Time to craft | Craft DC | Cost to craft |
| ------------- | ----------- | ------------- | -------- | ------------- |
| Basic         | 3           | 1 day         | -10      | 100 gold      |
| Expert        | 5           | 3 days        | -20      | 500 gold      |
| Advanced      | 8           | 1 week        | -30      | 1000 gold     |
| Master        | 12          | 2 weeks       | -40      | 2000 gold     |
| Dual*         | 5/5         | 1 week        | -30      | 1000 gold     |
| Advanced Dual | 8/8         | 2 weeks       | -40      | 2000 gold     |
| Master Dual   | 12/12       | 4 weeks       | -50      | 4000 gold     |
*a specific talent is required to craft dual talismans

see [[Crafting]] for rules on  how to craft.

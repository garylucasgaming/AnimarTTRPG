#### Rune Smith
Prerequisite:   `=this.requisite`

 whenever you  cast a channeled spell, you  do not need to concentrate on that spell for subsequent turns. the amount of channeled spells you can have going is equal to your  [[Intellect]] modifier / 3.   the spells still consume mana each turn they are active.

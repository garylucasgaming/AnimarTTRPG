#### Witch's Cauldron
Prerequisite:   `=this.requisite`

 gain a magical cauldron. This cauldron allows the witch to brew magical effects. These effects permeate the area within a 15ft radius around the witch.  as a bonus action the witch can add 1 ingredient to the cauldron, augmenting the effects of the cauldron. the same ingredient cannot be more than once. 

At the end of an encounter, the cauldrons effects subside. 

###### Cauldron Effects

| ingredient       | effect                                                                                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| wool of bat      | allies in the mist gain 1 damage reduction                                                  |
| eye of newt      | enemy attacks in the mist have a 2% chance to miss.                                         |
| toe of frog      | allies heal 1 damage per turn in the mist.                                                  |
| adder's fork     | enemies gain 1 level of fatigue in the mist.                                                |
| blind-worm       | the mist thickens, decreasing visibility. ranged attacks get -5 to hit targets in the mist. |
| four leaf clover | allies in the mist get +5 to all tests.                                                     |

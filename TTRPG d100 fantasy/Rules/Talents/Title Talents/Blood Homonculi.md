#### Blood Homonculi
Prerequisite:   `=this.requisite`

 as a standard action, by expending 3 blood of the same type from their amulet, they can create a small creature who shares the same blood type(thus the same mana type) of the blood used. Blood homunculi are always willing to share their blood with the blood mage. A blood homunculi lasts for 24 hours before collapsing into a pile of coagulated blood. only one blood homunculi of each blood type can be sustained by the amulet at any time.

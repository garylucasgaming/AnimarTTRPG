#### Craft Rune Stones
Prerequisite: `=this.requisite`

Gain [[Craft]](Rune Stones) as a trained skill. Rune stones can be used to store spells by infusing the mana needed into the stone itself. rune stones can only be used once and are destroyed in the process of using them.

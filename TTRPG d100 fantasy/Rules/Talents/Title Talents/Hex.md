#### Hex
Prerequisite:   `=this.requisite`

As a bonus action, a witch may cast a hex targeting another creature. The witch must make an [[Arcane Arts]] roll to cast the hex. 

The Target creature can make a [[Education]] check on their turn to attempt to remove the hex. They must beat the Degrees of success the Witch got when the witch cast the hex. 

A witch can only maintain 1 hex at a time.  

###### Hexes

| Hex                     | effect                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Toil                    | target gains 1 level of fatigue/round                              |
| Bubbling miasma         | target makes fortitude test or becomes poisoned.                   |
| Discern Truths and lies | gain +10 [[Insight]] against target creature                       |
| Enfeebling hex          | target gets -10 on all [[Strength]] and [[Dexterity]] based skills |
| The Black Mark          | attacks made against target get +10                                |

#### Aura of Command
Prerequisite:   `=this.requisite`

as a standard action, Roll a [[Persuade]] check. 

if you fail nothing happens. 

On success allies withing 15 feet of you gain a +10 bonus to all skill and characteristic rolls. 

each turns roll a [[Persuade]] check. if it fails the aura ends.

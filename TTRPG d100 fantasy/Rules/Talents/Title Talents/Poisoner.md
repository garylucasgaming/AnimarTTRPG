#### Poisoner
Prerequisite:   `=this.requisite`

gain [[Craft]](poisons).

You may coat the blade or tip of a weapon with poison, and on the next successful attack that does damage after damage reduction, the poison is removed from the weapon and the effect is applied to the target. 

When a target drinks or is hit with a weapon that does damage after damage reduction, they must roll a [[Fortitude]] check or become poisoned with the effect. Each turn they may roll a [[Fortitude]] check to remove the effect of the poison. 
Poison will last 3 rounds or until the poison test is beaten.

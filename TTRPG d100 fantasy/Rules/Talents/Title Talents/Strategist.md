#### Strategist
Prerequisite: `=this.requisite`


once per turn, the scoundrel can choose from a list of abilities to apply. 

| ability          | effect                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| distract         | as a reaction the scoundrel shouts something that distracts their assailant. on a successful [[Deceive]] test vs [[Insight]], the scoundrel is able to dodge an attack that wouldve otherwise hit.        |
| defensive roll   | as a reaction, if an opponent would hit the rogue with a melee attack, the scoundrel can roll an [[Evasion Skill]] with +20, to move up to 5 ft and take half damage.(uncanny dodge does not effect this) |
| pocket sand      | standard action scoundrel makes a [[Weapon Skill]](unarmed strike) on success, opponent gets -10 to attacks for 1 turn.                                                                                   |
| Call out         | bonus action, call out an opponent within 15 ft, allies making ranged attacks gain a +10 bonus to hit them.                                                                                               |
| crippling strike | when the scoundrel makes a sneak attack, the opponent makes a [[Fortitude]] or gains 1 level of fatigue                                                                                                   |

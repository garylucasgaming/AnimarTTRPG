#### Siphon Blood
Prerequisite:   `=this.requisite`

this is a standard action, and it lets the blood mage extract blood from those around it. each non willing creature within 15 feet must roll a [[Fortitude]] test or the blood mage gets to drain some of their blood. this blood is immediately transferred into the blood amulet.

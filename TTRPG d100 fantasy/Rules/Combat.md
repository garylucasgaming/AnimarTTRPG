# Combat



## how combat works

Fundamentally, combat in this game is just a [[Skills]] test. However it is an opposing [[Skills]] test against your opponent.  

You roll your weapon skill with the weapon you are choosing to attack with, and your opponent can choose to try and evade(using the evasion skill as a reaction), or not evade. 

If they evade, you roll your [[weapon skill]], and they roll their [[evasion skill]]. and the degrees of success are used to determine if you hit or not. If you have more degrees of success than your opponent, you win the roll and hit them. If not, they evade the attack. Your degrees of success over your opponent determine severity of attack. 

If they chose not to evade the attack, you still roll your weapon skill, and if you succeed you hit them. Your degrees of success determine severity of attack. 

> [!Tip]+ Example of Attack roll. 
> Johnny wants to attack with his Great Sword. Great swords are strength based so Weapon Skill(Great sword) will be based of the strength characteristic. Johnny has 47 in strength, which means he has a strength modifier of 4. his weapon skill(Great sword) will then have a base value of 40. (strength modifier of 4 X 10 when trained in this skill). 
> to attack with his great sword, johnny rolls a d100, and he gets a 25. 25 is lower than his value of 40, so he succeeds his roll. Johnny also rolled 15 points lower than his value, which would give him 1 degree of success. 
> Johnny's opponent then rolls an evasion skill test, to try and dodge the attack. They roll a 60, and their skill value is 30. they fail their roll and johnny successfully hits his opponent with a weak hit. 

### Iniatiative

initiate is determine by rolling a [[Dexterity]] test. your number determines your place in initiative. the lower the number the faster your character moves in turn order. if there is a tie, it is determined by degrees of success.

Once you have Hit, you determine which body part you hit(unless making a precision attack). 

to determine body part the 1's column of your hit roll is used. 

| 1's | body part |
| --- | --------- |
| 1-2 | Leg       |
| 3-5 | Arm       |
| 6-9 | Torso     |
| 10  | Head      |

For arms and legs the ones column is also used to determine right or left. 


| d10           | effect                     |
| ------------- | -------------------------- |
| 1             | Right                      |
| 2             | Left                       |
| 3             | Right                      |
| 4             | Left                       |
| 5+10's column | if odd right, if even left |

### Damage

There are a few different types of damage in the game. 

- bludgeoning
- piercing 
- slashing 
- fire
- cold
- lightning
- light
- decay
- acid


Each weapon and spell will have a different type of damage associated with it. A Greatsword for instance might use slashing, where as a Bolt of lightning would deal lightning damage.  


### Wound tables 

When applying negatives to [[Skills]] or [[Characteristics]], if hit in the head, it will always effect [[Intellect]] and or [[Education]]. otherwise, other parts of the body effect [[Dexterity]] and or [[Strength]]. 

Wounds can be treated during combat, either with magic spells, or the [[First Aid]] skill. see [[Wounds and Treatment]] for how the first aid skill can be used to treat certain wounds, and the long term care and effects of such wounds.
